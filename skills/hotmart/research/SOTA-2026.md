  `/co`, `/mx`, `/la`). Offices on the footer: Belo Horizonte, Madrid,

## 2. Eligibility (the #133 gate — EC-relevant facts)

- ToU §A: "These terms govern the access or use of the resources contained on
  the Platform for all Users, anywhere in the world. Certain features might
  not be available in all territories and to all Users." [OFICIAL S2]
- ToU §H: the only territorial exclusions are sanctions-based — OFAC (US
  Office of Foreign Assets Control), the UK Sanctions List, the UN Security
  Council Sanctions List. Ecuador is not on those lists. [OFICIAL S2]
  [INF: eligibility as written covers an Ecuador-resident creator; the payout
  rail (§4 below) is the open datum, not eligibility.]
- ToU §2.8 (Banking Information): after the first sale the creator must
  provide banking information "indicating the bank account in their name";
  registering accounts "located outside the Creator…'s country of residence
  is also prohibited". [OFICIAL S2]
- Contracting entity varies by residence/currency: Hotmart BR (BRL between
  Brazil-resident users), Hotmart USA (USD/US), Hotmart BV (NL) for
  everything else — an EC creator contracts with Hotmart BV. [OFICIAL S2 §C]
- ToU §1.4 ("No Promise of Performance, Gain, or Results from the Use of the
  Platform"): Hotmart does not promise results, and neither may creators or
  affiliates — "no Creator, Co-producer or Affiliate can promise any
  performance, gain, or an unobtainable result of any nature". This is the
  clause behind the skill's copy rule (SKILL.md §Regla de oro — no
  results-promising prose). [OFICIAL S2 §1.4]

## 3. Fees (Payment Policy, version updated 2026-09-21 — fetched 2026-10-05)

The two creator fees are charged cumulatively, only when a product sells. [OFICIAL S3 §5.1]

| Fee | Value | Scope | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fixed Fee per Transaction | BRL 2.49 / USD 1.00 / EUR 1.00 | by the BUYER's payment currency (Table 1) | [OFICIAL S3 §5.2] |
| Service Fee | 20% of the product price up to BRL 10.00; **9.9%** from BRL 10.01 | §5.3 states the threshold in BRL; §5.4 generalizes: offers ≤ USD 15 / EUR 15 are micro-transactions — fixed fee USD 0.10 / EUR 0.10 + 9.9% | [OFICIAL S3 §5.3–§5.4] |
| SeQura (ES installment method) | 1% of the sales price | only for Spanish buyers, SeQura-registered creators | [OFICIAL S3 §8.3] |
| Withdrawal minimum (BRL) | BRL 2.00 + a BRL 1.99 withdrawal fee | BRL offers — FAQ #08; §12 (the binding text) was truncated in today's fetch, so treat this as the FAQ figure | [OFICIAL S9 FAQ #08] |
| Withdrawal deadlines | BRL sales: 30 calendar days after the sale (PIX/Boleto/Hotmart balance: ~2 calendar days); other currencies: 15 calendar days (warranty period) | FAQ #08; §12 truncated | [OFICIAL S9 FAQ #08] |

Caveat: §5.3's threshold is BRL-denominated even for other offer currencies;
§5.4's micro-transaction table is the USD/EUR generalization. A future
surface-fee claim should quote §5.4 for USD/EUR offers, §5.3 for BRL.

## 4. Payout rail — the deciding datum is still `[?]`

- What is known: after the first sale, the creator registers a bank account
  in their own name in their country of residence (ToU §2.8 — third-party and
  out-of-residence accounts are prohibited). Hotpay processes payments
  "anywhere in the world" (Payment Policy §A) with currency conversion rules
  (§4); withdrawal minimums/deadlines are in the FAQ (§3 table, S9 FAQ #08) —
