## 1. What Hotmart is — platform identity

- Digital-products storefront + memberships + affiliate program; the largest
  digital-products storefront for the Spanish/Portuguese-speaking markets.
  Company: Brazil/Spain-based; offices listed on the pricing footer: Belo
  Horizonte, Madrid, Ámsterdam, Bogotá, Ciudad de México, Nueva York.
  [OFICIAL S1]
- Memberships: the Payment Policy's recurring billing "may also be called a
  'subscription'"; Members Area is a first-class product surface (pricing nav:
  "Members area"). [OFICIAL S1, S3 §9.1]
- Pricing model: "No monthly or annual fees. No limits to selling around the
  world. Just pay a transaction fee on each sale you make." [OFICIAL S1]

## 2. Eligibility (the #133 gate — EC-relevant facts)

- ToU §A: "These terms govern the access or use of the resources contained on
  the Platform for all Users, anywhere in the world. Certain features might
  not be available in all territories and to all Users." [OFICIAL S2]
- ToU §H: the only territorial exclusions are sanctions-based — OFAC (US
  Office of Foreign Assets Control), the UK Sanctions List, the UN Security
  Council Sanctions List. Ecuador is not on those lists. [OFICIAL S2]
  [INF: eligibility as written covers an Ecuador-resident creator; the payout
  rail (§4 below) is the open datum, not eligibility.]
- ToU §2.8 (Banking Information): after the first sale the creator must
  provide banking information "indicating the bank account in their name";
  registering accounts "located outside the Creator…'s country of residence
  is also prohibited". [OFICIAL S2]
- Contracting entity varies by residence/currency: Hotmart BR (BRL between
  Brazil-resident users), Hotmart USA (USD/US), Hotmart BV (NL) for
  everything else — an EC creator contracts with Hotmart BV. [OFICIAL S2 §C]

## 4. Payout rail — the deciding datum is still `[?]`

- What is known: after the first sale, the creator registers a bank account
  in their own name in their country of residence (ToU §2.8 — third-party and
  out-of-residence accounts are prohibited). Hotpay processes payments
  "anywhere in the world" (Payment Policy §A) with currency conversion rules
  (§4) and withdrawal deadlines (§12 — truncated). [OFICIAL S2, S3]
